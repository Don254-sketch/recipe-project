Kenyan Fried Tilapia

A crispy whole tilapia simmered in a rich tomato, onion, garlic and dhania sauce. It's especially good with ugali and sukuma wiki.

Ingredients

Instructions

  1. Heat oil in a pan over medium heat.
  2. Add the whole tilapia and cook for 5–7 minutes on each side, or until golden brown.
  3. Add chopped onions, tomatoes and garlic.
  4. Add dhania and simmer until the sauce thickens.
  5. Serve hot with ugali and sukuma wiki.
